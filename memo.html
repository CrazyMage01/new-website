Making my skillset and interests legible: strengths, skills, growth areas, and how I work. A living document; poking holes in it is a favour.

Strengths

  • approachable, and good at getting people to think clearly through targeted questioning

  • open to non-conventional career moves and strangely ambitious things if they seem impactful

Skills

  • Writing writes Samstack and co-writes the Fieldbuilders Blog; a handful of EA organisers changed their strategy because of one forum post

Growth areas

  • actively making my skillset and outputs more legible through consistent writing and publicly shared projects
  • strongest in the early and middle phases of projects; building better systems for taking things cleanly over the finish line
  • thrive with accountability structures and in-person collaboration, so I'm intentional about building those into my working environment

How I work

  • extrovert: I get energy and motivation from people, and need at least some in-person aspect to my work
  • happiest in radically transparent, flatly managed teams; heavy bureaucracy would be a poor fit
  • I need to buy the leadership and feel good about the culture of the team I work in
